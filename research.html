Research.

Learning theoryKernel methodsFunctional data analysisInverse problems

My research focuses on general regularization techniques for ill-posed inverse problems within learning theory.

Tikhonov regularization is widely studied across learning-theoretic frameworks, but its qualification imposes limitations. I study more general regularization methods to obtain improved qualification and convergence rates.

Research interests

  • Regularization methods and ill-posed inverse problems
  • Learning theory and convergence rates
  • Functional linear regression
  • Kernel methods and general source conditions

Research themes

Functional linear regression

Convergence analysis and optimal rates for functional linear regression using kernel conjugate gradient, general regularization, and regularised Nyström methods.

Learning over Hilbert spaces

General source conditions and their role in the theoretical analysis of learning algorithms.
