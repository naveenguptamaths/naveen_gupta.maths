ABOUT

Mathematics meets
statistical learning.

I am Naveen Gupta, currently a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Statistics at The Pennsylvania State University, University Park, working with Prof. Bharath K. Sriperumbudur.

Prior to joining Penn State, I pursued both my Master's and Ph.D. degrees in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, completing my doctorate in 2025 under the supervision of Dr. S. Sivananthan. My doctoral thesis was titled “Convergence Analysis of Regularized Learning Algorithms for Functional Data.”

RESEARCH / QUESTIONS & METHODS

Learning from functions.
Finding structure in data.

I study theoretical and computational aspects of infinite-dimensional learning, with a focus on regularization, functional data analysis, and kernel methods.

01 / REGULARIZATION
λ

Making inverse problems tractable

General regularization methods for ill-posed learning problems, with improved qualification and convergence rates.

02 / FUNCTIONAL DATA
ƒ

Learning with curves and functions

Functional linear and nonlinear regression, kernel conjugate gradient, and regularised Nyström methods.

03 / LEARNING THEORY
ℋ

Understanding why methods work

Kernel methods, general source conditions, and convergence analysis for learning over Hilbert spaces.
